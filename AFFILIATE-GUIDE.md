# AliExpress Affiliate: Registration & Approval Guide

Official portal: https://portals.aliexpress.com (AliExpress's own program). Alternatives if you are rejected: Awin, CJ, FlexOffers, Admitad (check which accept your country).

## Before you apply
1. Create a normal AliExpress buyer account with a real name and email (verify email and phone).
2. Build your site first (this project) and add 15-30 real products so reviewers see a genuine, working site. Use the free `.netlify.app` URL.
3. Add an affiliate disclosure (already in the footer) and a short About page.
4. Open social pages for the shop (Instagram/TikTok/Facebook/WhatsApp channel) with real posts. Reviewers check promotion channels.

## Apply
1. Go to portals.aliexpress.com, click **Join now / Sign up**, log in with your AliExpress account.
2. Fill the form: name, email, verify with the code sent to you.
3. Add your promotion channel(s): website URL + social links. Describe your niche honestly (e.g. "gadgets and home deals for Nigerian shoppers").
4. Accept the terms and submit. Review usually takes a few days to a week; some applicants wait longer or get no reply.
5. Add payout details when asked. Direct payouts are by international bank transfer; the minimum is reportedly around $16. Confirm in the Portals dashboard.

## After approval
1. Portals > **Promo Tools > Link Generator**: paste any product URL to get your affiliate link; paste that into the admin panel.
2. Create a **Tracking ID** per channel to see which one earns.
3. Check that each product's seller participates in the program, otherwise no commission is recorded.

## If rejected or ignored
- Wait 2 weeks, improve the site (more products, content, social followers), and re-apply.
- Try Awin/CJ/FlexOffers, which approve AliExpress promotion through their network.

## Rules to avoid a ban
- Always disclose affiliate links. Never click your own links to buy or use fake traffic/spam. Don't bid on AliExpress brand terms in ads. Don't show wrong prices; send people to AliExpress for the final price.
- Commission varies by category (publicly reported roughly 3% to 9%, "Hot Deals" can be 0%), so verify current rates in the dashboard.
